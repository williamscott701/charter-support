Charter turns an exam date and the hours you actually have into a schedule, then keeps it honest when the weeks don't go to plan. Here's how it works, and what to do when it doesn't.

How does Charter decide what to study today?

Three inputs. The exam's own published weight for each topic sets the baseline, so a topic worth 15% of the paper gets roughly triple the hours of one worth 5%. Your self-rated confidence then bends that: the topic you rated 0 pulls up to about 1.8 times what its weight alone would give it, and the one you rated 5 gets exactly its weighted share and no less — nobody should walk in having spent zero hours on anything.

Those hours are then spread across the days you said you're free, interleaved so no topic is finished in one block and forgotten, with the last stretch before the exam reserved for review and mock papers rather than new material.

What happens when I fall behind?

Nothing accumulates. Charter does not show you a growing pile of missed days — that is the thing that makes people delete study apps. Instead it re-cuts the days you have left around the work that is genuinely still outstanding, re-weighted by how you feel about each topic now.

This happens automatically once you have drifted more than six hours from the plan, or on demand from the rebuild button on the Plan tab. Hours you have already logged are always kept. You can turn the automatic version off in Settings.

It says my plan "won't fit" — what does that mean?

It means the honest thing: the hours still to do are more than the days before your exam can hold at the availability you entered. Charter tells you this as early as it can see it, rather than quietly overfilling days you were never going to work.

You have three real options, and the app will show you the numbers for each: add hours to your weekly availability, reduce your target hours, or accept it and let the schedule prioritise by exam weight so the shortfall comes off the topics worth least. Any of those is better than finding out in the last fortnight.

Are the topic weights official?

They follow the published outline for the curriculum year shown in the app, but exam boards revise them, and Charter ships with them marked unverified for exactly that reason — you will see a notice on the Topics tab saying so.

Check them against the official study session outline for your own sitting. Charter is a planner, not an authority on the syllabus. If the weights have moved, the split of your hours will be slightly off, and that is worth knowing before you rely on it.

Does Charter include practice questions or study notes?

No, and it never will. Charter contains no exam content of any kind. It plans and tracks your time; your curriculum, question bank and notes come from wherever you already get them. That's a deliberate scope, not a missing feature.

What is the readiness score, and should I trust it?

It combines three things per topic: how much of the allocated time you have actually done (55%), how confident you have rated yourself (30%), and how recently you touched it (15%, halving roughly every three weeks). Those are weighted by what each topic is worth to the exam.

Treat it as a planning aid, not a prediction. It is built from numbers you gave it about yourself, so it is exactly as honest as your own ratings. It cannot tell you whether you will pass — nothing can. The projection next to it shows where the score lands on exam day if your recent pace holds, which is usually the more useful of the two.

Where is my data, and how do I get it out?

On your device, and nowhere else — no account, no server. Charter Pro exports every session and mock score as CSV from Settings → Data. Settings also has a Delete all data option that removes everything immediately. Full detail in the privacy policy.

My widget isn't updating

Widgets refresh when the app writes a new summary — which happens whenever you log a session or change the plan — and again after midnight so the countdown stays right. If one looks stale:

  • Open Charter once; that forces a refresh.
  • Remove the widget and add it again.
  • Check Settings → General → Background App Refresh is on.

The readiness widget shows a lock without a subscription; that is expected, not a fault.

Reminders aren't arriving

Charter uses local notifications only, so there is no server to blame. Check Settings → Notifications → Charter is allowed, that Focus or Scheduled Summary is not holding them back, and that the daily reminder is switched on inside Charter's own Settings. Changing the reminder time reschedules everything.

Pro, trials, and cancelling

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, annual or monthly, billed by Apple. The countdown, your next session and manual logging stay free forever, and nothing you have logged is ever locked away if a subscription ends.

Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or via the Manage subscription link in Charter's Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Pro until the period you paid for runs out. Refunds are Apple's to give — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Restored a device or changed phone? Tap Restore purchases in Settings.
