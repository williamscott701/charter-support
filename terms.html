2. What Charter is, and what it is not

Charter takes an exam date, the hours you can study each week and how confident you feel about each topic, and turns them into a dated schedule. It tracks the hours you log against that schedule, scores how ready you appear to be, and re-cuts the plan when you fall behind.

Charter is not a course, a question bank, a tutor or a source of exam content. It contains no curriculum material, no practice questions and no study notes. It is a calendar and a tracker. The topic names and published weight bands it uses to divide your hours are factual references to publicly available exam outlines, and you should confirm them against the official outline for your own sitting — the app says so where they are shown.

Charter is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or associated with CFA Institute or any other examining body, and none of them has reviewed, approved or contributed to it.
