Charter Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Charter is a study planner for the CFA® exams. It keeps your exam date, your study hours and your self-ratings on your device, and sends them nowhere.

In one paragraph

Charter has no account system, no server and no analytics. Everything you enter stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. The developer cannot see your study log, your readiness score or your exam date, because none of it is ever transmitted. The only network requests Charter makes are to Apple, to check whether your subscription is active.

1. The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • There is no account, no login and no user profile.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter.
  • Nothing you enter is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data with it.

2. No accounts, no sign-in

Charter does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. There is nothing to sign in to. Your Apple Account is used only by Apple, to process a subscription if you buy one — Charter never sees your Apple Account details.

3. What Charter stores, and where

Charter stores the following on your device, and only on your device:

  • The exam and level you selected, and your exam date.
  • Your target hours and the hours you have said you can study each day of the week.
  • Your self-rated confidence for each topic.
  • Each study session you log: topic, date, length, type and any note you write.
  • Any mock exam scores you record.
  • The generated schedule, and app preferences such as reminder times.

This lives in the app's private container, which iOS protects from other apps, plus a shared container used so the widgets can read a small summary. None of it leaves the device.

4. Widgets

Charter's widgets run in a separate process from the app, so the app writes a small summary file — days remaining, today's sessions, your readiness score and recent hours — into a shared container that both can read. That file stays on your device. Anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen can see whatever the widget displays, which is worth knowing before adding one.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. Nothing is sent through a push server, and the developer has no way to send you a notification. Notification text is composed on your device and may include today's topic and your countdown, so it can appear on your Lock Screen. Turn reminders off in Settings, or revoke the permission in iOS Settings, at any time.

6. Subscriptions and payment

The Full Edition is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the entire transaction; Charter never sees or stores your payment details. The app asks Apple whether an active subscription exists for this Apple Account and receives back an entitlement — nothing that identifies you personally. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

7. Exporting and sharing your data

The Full Edition can export your sessions and mock scores as CSV files. The file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet, where you choose what happens to it. If you send it somewhere — email, a messaging app, a cloud drive — that destination's own privacy policy applies from then on. Charter has no involvement once you have chosen.

8. Device backups

Because Charter's data lives in your app container, it is included in iCloud backups and encrypted local backups if you have those turned on. Those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's terms, not by Charter.

9. No tracking, analytics, or advertising

Charter contains no analytics, no advertising, no attribution SDK, no fingerprinting and no third-party trackers of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and never presents an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track. No data is shared with data brokers.

10. Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete all data option that removes every session, rating, mock score and plan immediately. Deleting the app from your device also removes everything. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing to request deletion of from anyone else.

11. Your rights, wherever you are

Privacy laws including the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA and Australia's Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal information, and to object to its processing.

Charter is built so those rights are exercised directly in the app rather than by request: you can view everything you have entered, edit or delete any of it, and export it as CSV. The developer acts as data controller only in the narrow sense of publishing the app; because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no stored copy to disclose, rectify, port or erase. Charter does not sell or share personal information, and has never done so.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can contact the developer at the address below, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

12. International users and data transfers

Charter performs no international transfer of your data, because it performs no transfer of your data at all. Everything stays on the device you entered it on. Purchases are processed by Apple in your own storefront under Apple's terms.

13. Children's privacy

Charter is a study planner for professional certification exams and is not directed at children. It is rated 4+ because it contains no objectionable content, but it collects no personal information from anyone, children included. No data is knowingly collected from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country) because no data is collected from any user.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes. Charter's design goal is that this policy stays this short.
